# Video Reel · Acne Scar Skin Layers · 30-45s

> **Format:** Vertical reel 9:16 · 30-45 seconds
> **Hero:** Animated skin cross-section diagram (4 layers reveal sequence)
> **Goal:** Authority + education + algorithm-friendly (high completion rate)
> **Channels:** TikTok · IG Reel · FB Reel · YouTube Shorts

---

## Concept

Animate diagram layer-by-layer · founder voiceover explains anatomy + technique · close with brand promise

→ Education that earns trust · ไม่ใช่ hard-sell

## Voice & Tone Direction

- **Voice:** หมอบิ๊ก (founder voice) · OR neutral male narrator if founder not available
- **Tone:** calm authority · ไม่ excited/sales-y · documentary feel
- **Pacing:** voice speed 1.0-1.2x (per brand voice rules) · ไม่เร็วเกิน
- **Inflection:** matter-of-fact · educational · slight warmth
- **Pause:** หลังแต่ละ layer reveal · ให้ audience absorb

---

## Visual Direction

- **Color grade:** espresso/brown base · bronze-gold highlights · cream text
- **Animation style:** smooth · editorial · ไม่ flashy
- **Transitions:** crossfade · slow zoom · NOT cuts/glitches
- **Typography:** Thai sans-serif (Noto Sans Thai / Prompt) · same as poster
- **Icons:** minimal · bronze-gold · same set as poster

---

## Audio Direction

- **Background music:** ambient cinematic · low-key · acoustic instrumental
  - Avoid: trap beats · pop · viral sounds (off-brand)
  - Prefer: documentary score · slow piano · soft strings
- **VO mixing:** clear · slight warmth · no auto-tune
- **No SFX** except subtle "whoosh" on layer reveals (optional)

## Production Notes

### **Option A · ทำเองด้วย Canva/CapCut + voiceover**
- Time: 2-4 hours
- Cost: free (if subscriptions in place)
- Quality: medium · acceptable for organic

### **Option B · HeyGen avatar + animated diagram**
- Time: 1-2 hours
- Cost: ~$30/mo subscription
- Quality: medium-high · consistent
- Use existing Dr.Big avatar from `assets/avatar/`

### **Option C · Motion designer freelance**
- Time: 3-7 days
- Cost: ฿15-30K per reel
- Quality: high · animation quality + sound design
- Worth it if it's hero asset / pinned post

→ **Recommendation:** Option B (HeyGen) สำหรับ MVP · Option C สำหรับ pinned post
